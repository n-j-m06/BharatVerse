## Overview

BharatVerse is an AI-powered Digital Twin platform designed to support data-driven governance, disaster management, urban planning, and policy decision-making.

The platform combines real-time analytics, AI simulations, geospatial visualization, and multi-agent intelligence into a unified command center for government authorities and emergency response teams.

Rather than relying solely on historical reports, BharatVerse enables administrators to simulate future scenarios, analyze risks, monitor national indicators, and receive AI-generated recommendations before making critical decisions.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern governance involves monitoring thousands of interconnected variables including transportation, weather, public safety, energy infrastructure, disasters, and citizen reports.

Most existing systems operate independently, forcing decision-makers to manually correlate information from multiple platforms.

BharatVerse addresses this challenge by creating a centralized AI-powered Digital Twin capable of integrating multiple datasets and intelligent agents into a single decision-support system.
